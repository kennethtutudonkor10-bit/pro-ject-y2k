About Ghana AI Lab

Learn about our mission, vision, and the team behind Ghana's AI revolution

Our Story

Ghana AI Lab was founded by Kenneth Donkor-Tutu with a vision to harness the power of artificial intelligence to solve local challenges and drive development in Ghana and across Africa.

We believe that AI has the potential to transform key sectors including education, healthcare, agriculture, and governance. Our approach combines cutting-edge technology with deep local understanding to create solutions that are both innovative and practical.

Our Vision

To position Ghana as a leader in AI innovation in Africa, creating sustainable solutions that improve lives and drive economic growth.

Our Mission

To develop and deploy AI solutions that address Ghana's unique challenges while building local capacity through research, training, and collaboration.
